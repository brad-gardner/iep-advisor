What prior written notice is

Prior written notice (PWN) is the letter your district must send before it changes — or refuses to change — Jacob's identification, evaluation, placement or services. In Ohio it's form PR-01.

  • What the district proposes or refuses, and why — specific to Jacob, not boilerplate
  • What data the decision was based on, and what else the team considered
